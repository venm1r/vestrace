# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Vestrace is committed to providing a welcoming, professional, and harassment-free environment for contributors and users, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or personal characteristics.

Participation in this repository means treating other people with respect and focusing discussion on ideas, evidence, and the work itself.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that supports a healthy community include:

- being respectful and constructive in reviews and discussions;
- assuming good intent while still asking for evidence and clarification;
- giving actionable technical feedback instead of attacking the person who proposed a change;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them;
- respecting project scope, maintainer decisions, and documented contribution rules;
- protecting private, security-sensitive, or personally identifying information.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable in project spaces:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory language;
- personal insults, sustained hostility, or deliberately inflammatory behavior;
- sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- spam, coordinated disruption, or intentionally derailing technical discussions;
- exploiting security reports, private disclosures, or moderation information to harm others;
- retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith conduct concern.

Disagreement about architecture, evidence, scope, or implementation is normal. It must remain focused on the work rather than the person.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, repository discussions, project-related communication, and other spaces where someone is representing the Vestrace project.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may edit or remove comments, close or lock conversations, reject contributions, restrict participation, or take other proportionate moderation action when necessary.

When deciding on enforcement, maintainers should consider context, severity, recurrence, impact, and whether the behavior appears intentional or correctable.

## Reporting

Project-level conduct concerns are currently handled by the repository owner, [@venm1r](https://github.com/venm1r). When a concern can be raised safely in the relevant thread, identify the behavior without escalating the conflict.

For abuse that should not be handled publicly, use GitHub's private platform reporting mechanisms where applicable. Do not publish sensitive personal information merely to document a conduct complaint.

Security vulnerabilities are not conduct reports and should be handled according to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Good-faith reports

Reports made in good faith will not be penalized merely because an investigation does not confirm a violation. Knowingly false or malicious reports may themselves be treated as a conduct violation.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by common open-source community codes of conduct, including the Contributor Covenant, while being tailored to the current size and governance of Vestrace.
